## Summary
Egged on by Danny, Chris pierces his left ear with a cheap fake-diamond stud whose post Danny filed sharp, then clips the post with wire cutters; the act arouses him, and the stone looks like a girl's earring. Next morning Mom, short on money, presses him to find a summer job and mentions Ms. Ravensworth hires students. At school Amber Lowell catches him staring at her pantyhose and tells him to apply; she works in the house. For the interview Chris uses Mom's white-flower conditioner and ties his hair in a low ponytail. Rosalina, warm and beautiful in off-black sheer pantyhose he can't stop looking at, interviews him first, offers the assistant gardener job and sets the dress code. Ms. Ravensworth, about to refuse him over the single earring, smells his hair, smiles, and hires him on condition he pierce the other ear. Leaving, he sees Amber in uniform, imagines the second needle, and has to hide his erection. On his first morning, owing Mom $72, he arrives with two diamonds; Ms. Ravensworth nods, warns the garden will be hard and that if it is, he must come to her. He takes the shovel with clean hands.
