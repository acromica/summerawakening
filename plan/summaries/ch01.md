# ch01 — The Left Ear

## Summary
Egged on by Danny, Chris pierces his own left ear with a cheap "diamond" stud whose post Danny filed sharp; the act arouses him, and the stone looks like a girl's earring. Next morning Mom, short on money, presses him to find a summer job, calls him a hoodlum, and mentions Ms. Ravensworth hires students. At school Amber Lowell catches him staring at her pantyhose and tells him to apply; she works in the house. For the interview Mom has him condition and brush his hair; he loves how it falls, and ties it in a low ponytail with her hair tie. Ms. Ravensworth questions him, calls him moldable, hires him as assistant gardener, and orders him to remove the earring or pierce the other ear. Leaving, he sees Amber in her maid's uniform, imagines a second piercing, and has to hide his erection. On his first morning, owing Mom 2, he arrives with the earring still in; Ms. Ravensworth warns that if the garden proves too hard he must come to her. He takes the shovel with clean hands.
