## Summary
Egged on by Danny, Chris pierces his left ear with a cheap fake-diamond stud whose post Danny filed sharp, then clips the post with wire cutters; the act arouses him. Mom, short on money, presses him to find a summer job and mentions Ms. Ravensworth. At school Amber Lowell catches him staring at her pantyhose and tells him to apply. On interview day he uses Mom's white-flower conditioner and rides his bike up Crest Road. Rosalina, warm and beautiful in off-black sheer pantyhose, sends his bike to the garden house, interviews him, and sets the dress code. Ms. Ravensworth, about to refuse him over the earring, smells his hair and hires him on condition he gets two. Riding home he decides to buy Mom the $210 bracelet with his first paycheck, and, seeing his reflection, decides to keep the piercing. Alone at home he finds Mom's polishes (one pearl like Rosalina's nails), files the second earring's post with her nail file, pierces his right ear, and masturbates at the mirror thinking of Rosalina. Monday, Mom notices the two earrings; at the garden house Amber calls, "Nice earrings, Chris!"
